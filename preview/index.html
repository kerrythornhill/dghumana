DG Humana Institute is an emerging independent initiative concerned with how technology can support human dignity, meaningful work, and the capacity to act.

Our purpose ↓

01 / Purpose

Human dignity belongs
at the center of progress.

Our purpose is to advance human dignity and agency in the age of automation.

Technology changes how people work, learn, make decisions, and participate in society. DG Humana exists to examine those changes with care—and to connect thoughtful inquiry with the practical choices organizations and communities face.

The guiding question is simple: how can technological capability expand people’s ability to understand, contribute, and shape their own lives?

02 / Vision

Progress measured
in human possibilities.

We envision a future in which innovation strengthens the conditions for people to flourish.

01

Dignity in work

Work that recognizes human contribution, supports learning, and makes room for purpose as technologies and roles change.

02

Agency in decisions

People able to understand, question, and influence the systems that shape their opportunities and everyday lives.

03

Knowledge in public

Ideas made accessible enough to inform thoughtful dialogue across research, professional practice, and civic life.

03 / Direction

Inquiry that reaches
beyond disciplines.

Our developing direction brings together ethics, the study of institutions, and attention to the realities of work and emerging technology.

We aim to create space for research, clear public writing, and conversations across academic, nonprofit, civic, and professional communities. The ambition is to make difficult questions more intelligible and the choices ahead more deliberate.

A future of greater capability.
A future with room for human judgment.
